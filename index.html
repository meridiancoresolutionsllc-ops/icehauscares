Who We Are

Creating safe, positive spaces where people thrive.

Ice Haus Cares is the community-wellbeing arm of the broader Ice Haus family. We exist because every neighborhood deserves places where individuals and families can connect, learn, grow, and feel supported.

From youth safety and mental wellness to peer support and scholarships, our work is built around a simple belief: when people feel safe, seen, and supported, whole communities grow stronger together.

  • Safety first. We help young people and families build the knowledge and confidence to stay safe.
  • Wellness for all. Mental and physical wellbeing programming that is welcoming and stigma-free.
  • Opportunity that lasts. Peer support and scholarships that open doors to stronger futures.

Scholarships

Investing in stronger futures.

Ice Haus Cares plans to support students and young adults through scholarships that ease the cost of education and open doors to opportunity. Details below are a starting framework and may evolve as the program grows.

Who may be eligible

  • Students and young adults in the communities we serve
  • A demonstrated commitment to learning, growth, or community
  • Financial need and/or participation in our programs
  • Open to a range of educational and vocational pathways

What scholarships can support

  • Tuition, fees, and course materials
  • Certifications, training, and skill-building programs
  • Wellness and safety education milestones
  • Mentorship and continued growth opportunities

Application timeline

Applications Open

Program details and forms become available to the community.

Submission Deadline

Completed applications and supporting materials are due.

Review & Selection

Our committee reviews applications and selects recipients.

Awards Announced

Recipients are notified and celebrated by the community.
